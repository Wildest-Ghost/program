02风险／差距原因Risk / Gap reason

未承接 IC-6 5.3.5–5.3.7 对查阅 CRA 信贷资料的三项具体要求（密码至少每季更换、查阅日志最少载明目的/日期/人员、定期以内部日志与 CRA 帐单纪录比对异常），内规第11章仅以「按 IC-6 行事」概括引用，第15章只到「不得未获授权查阅」，缺可执行控制与比对机制。Fails to carry over the three specific requirements in IC-6 5.3.5–5.3.7 on access to CRA credit data (quarterly password change, access log capturing at least purpose/date/staff, periodic reconciliation of internal logs against CRA billing records); Chapter 11 only refers generally to IC-6 and Chapter 15 stops at prohibiting unauthorised access, leaving no executable control or reconciliation mechanism.
内规对 CRA 查阅仅作「应按 Code / IC-6 / MCRA COBP 行事」的原则性指引，未把 IC-6 的三项动作落成本行程序：(1) 无密码定期（至少每季）更换频率；(2) 无查阅日志的最少字段要求（目的、日期、查阅人员），因而无法作为符合 Code 的证据；(3) 无「以本行内部查阅纪录与 CRA 提供资料（含帐单纪录）定期比对、识别异常查阅量或无合理解释差异」的检测动作。CRA 查阅控制整体缺位，一旦发生员工滥用查阅，本行既无法事前约束、也无法事后举证。The Guidelines provide only a general direction that CRA access should follow the Code / IC-6 / MCRA Code of Practice, without translating IC-6’s three actions into local procedures: (1) no frequency for password change (at least quarterly); (2) no minimum field set for the access log (purpose, date, staff), so it cannot serve as evidence of Code compliance; (3) no detection step to periodically compare internal access records with information available from CRAs (including billing records) for unusual volume or unexplained discrepancies. Overall control over CRA access is absent, so staff abuse can be neither prevented nor evidenced.

03改进意见Recommendations

在第11章下增设「CRA 查阅控制」程序：明确系统密码至少每季更换；规定查阅日志必备字段（查阅目的、查阅日期时间、查阅人员编号）；规定按月或按季将内部查阅日志与 CRA 提供的查阅及帐单纪录比对，异常量或无合理解释差异须由部门主管复核并留存调查结论。Add a “CRA access control” procedure under Chapter 11: quarterly password change at minimum; mandatory access-log fields (purpose, date/time, staff ID); monthly or quarterly reconciliation of internal access logs against CRA access and billing records, with supervisor review and documented investigation of unusual volume or unexplained discrepancies.

02风险／差距原因Risk / Gap reason

未承接 IC-6 5.4.1 与 5.4.3 对 CRA 信贷报告的使用限制与销毁要求：内规第16章只就身份证副本及一般客户文件设定保留期与销毁日志，未规定信贷报告仅限 need-to-know、复制与传阅受限，以及拒贷或客户终止借贷关系后须于合理期间内销毁。Does not carry over IC-6 5.4.1 and 5.4.3 on use restrictions and destruction of CRA credit reports: Chapter 16 only sets retention periods and an erasure log for IC copies and general customer documents, with no need-to-know restriction, no limits on copying/circulation, and no requirement to destroy reports within a reasonable period after a declined application or termination of the borrowing relationship.
内规的保留与销毁机制以「个人资料保留矩阵」为对象，覆盖身份证副本、未获批贷款申请文件等，但未把「向 CRA 取得的信贷报告」列为独立类别，因而三项要素均缺：(1) 信贷报告只限 need-to-know 查阅，无授权范围规定；(2) 报告的复制、抄录与传阅无限制；(3) 拒贷或客户不再有任何借贷关系时，须于合理期间内销毁（除另有准许用途）。信贷报告含完整负债与还款纪录，属最敏感客户资料之一，留存范围与销毁时点不明将同时构成私隐与举证风险。The retention and erasure regime is built around the “Data Retention Matrix” covering IC copies and declined loan application documents, but does not treat CRA credit reports as a separate category. Three elements are therefore missing: (1) no need-to-know access restriction on credit reports; (2) no limits on duplication, copying or circulation; (3) no requirement to destroy reports within a reasonable period where an application is declined or the borrowing relationship ends, other than for other permitted purposes. Credit reports contain full indebtedness and repayment records and are among the most sensitive customer data; unclear scope and timing of destruction creates both privacy and evidential risk.

03改进意见Recommendations

在保留矩阵中增列「CRA 信贷报告」类别，明确：仅限有信贷审批或复核需要的指定人员查阅；禁止未经授权复制、抄录或外传；贷款申请被拒或客户终止全部借贷关系后，除另有准许用途外须于设定期间内销毁，销毁纪录写入既有 Personal Data Erasure Logsheet（Appendix 24）。Add a “CRA credit report” category to the retention matrix specifying access limited to designated staff with a credit approval or review need; prohibition on unauthorised copying, transcription or circulation; and destruction within a defined period after a declined application or full termination of the borrowing relationship (unless needed for other permitted purposes), with destruction recorded in the existing Personal Data Erasure Logsheet (Appendix 24).

02风险／漏洞原因Risk / Loophole reason

未承接 IC-6 5.5.2、5.5.3、5.5.4 的账户资料更新时限：外规要求向 CRA 提供的账户资料须于不超过31天的每个申报期末前更新、客户提出更新要求后须尽快且不迟于规定时限更新，内规第11章及第16章全无更新时限与更新渠道（Credit Reference Platform）规定，仅有资料准确性的一般要求。Fails to carry over the update timelines in IC-6 5.5.2, 5.5.3 and 5.5.4: account data provided to CRAs must be updated by the end of each reporting period not exceeding 31 days, and updates requested by an individual must be made promptly and no later than the prescribed deadline; Chapters 11 and 16 contain no update timeline or channel (Credit Reference Platform) requirement, only a general accuracy obligation.
内规只有「确保个人资料准确」这一结果性要求，没有任何可执行的更新节奏：既无「每个不超过31天的申报期内更新账户资料」的期限，也无客户提出更正后「尽快且不迟于规定时限」的升级时限，更未指定须经 Credit Reference Platform 报送。后果是欠款结清、债务重组安排达成或账户注销等关键状态可能长期未反映于 CRA 数据库，直接影响客户在其他机构的信贷申请结果，属客户资料与重大交易影响的直接违反情形。The Guidelines contain only an outcome requirement to keep personal data accurate, with no executable updating cadence: no deadline to update account data within each reporting period not exceeding 31 days, no escalation deadline to act promptly and no later than the prescribed limit upon an individual’s correction request, and no designation of the Credit Reference Platform as the reporting channel. As a result, key statuses such as full settlement, execution of a debt restructuring arrangement or account termination may remain unreflected in CRA databases for extended periods, directly affecting customers’ credit applications elsewhere — a direct breach scenario affecting customer data and significant transactions.

03改进意见Recommendations

在第11章增设「CRA 资料更新」程序：明确每一申报期不超过31天、期末前经 Credit Reference Platform 完成更新；客户提出更新要求后的处理时限与升级路径；账户终止或撇账须即时更新标示；并指定责任部门与复核岗位。Add a “CRA data updating” procedure under Chapter 11: each reporting period not exceeding 31 days with updates completed before period end via the Credit Reference Platform; a handling deadline and escalation path after an individual’s update request; immediate update of termination or write-off status; and designated responsible and reviewing functions.

未承接 IC-6 5.7.1 与 5.7.2 的年度合规审计要求：外规要求至少每年就消费信贷资料的共用与使用进行合规审计，并须把评估整体有效性的审计报告提交董事会或指定机构，内规只有第3.13段内审定期审查与第19.2段年度检讨，未针对 CRA 资料设专门年度合规审计及董事会层级报告义务。Does not carry over the annual compliance audit requirement in IC-6 5.7.1 and 5.7.2: a compliance audit of the sharing and use of consumer credit data must be conducted at least annually and the report, assessing overall effectiveness, submitted to the Board or a designated authority; the Guidelines only provide for periodic internal audit review (3.13) and an annual review of the Guidelines themselves (19.2), with no dedicated annual compliance audit or Board-level reporting on CRA data.

The required audit report assessing overall effectiveness, covering security breaches, violations, management responses and improvement recommendations, and being submitted to the Board or a designated authority, has no counterpart.

03改进意见Recommendations

在第11章或第3章增设：每年至少一次由内审或独立合规职能就 CRA 资料共用与使用执行合规审计，出具评估整体有效性的报告，涵盖违规与安全事故、管理层回应及改善建议，并于完成后提交董事会或其指定委员会审阅。Add in Chapter 11 or 3: an annual (at least) compliance audit of CRA data sharing and use by internal audit or an independent compliance function, producing a report that assesses overall effectiveness and covers violations, security incidents, management responses and remediation, submitted to the Board or its designated committee upon completion.

未承接 IC-6 第6章关于「查阅前通知、删除选择权、逾期与结清提醒」的完整义务链（6.1 事先通知查阅意图、6.2 申请时告知删除结清账户资料的选择权、6.3 逾期30日内书面提醒、6.4 结清后提醒删除权、6.5 显著性与2年留存程序），内规第11章仅概括引用 IC-6，全文无任何通知程序。Does not carry over the notification chain in IC-6 Chapter 6 (6.1 prior notice of intention to access, 6.2 disclosure at application of the option to delete closed account data, 6.3 written reminder within 30 days of default, 6.4 reminder of the deletion right upon full repayment, 6.5 prominence, procedures and two-year record keeping); Chapter 11 refers generally to IC-6 and there is no notification procedure anywhere in the Guidelines.
